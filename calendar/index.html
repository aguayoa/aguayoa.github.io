Click the calendar for Year, Overview, Reminders and To-do. Click a day to write its summary; drag across days to make an event; click a day's colored dots to see its reminders. Overview shows the whole year, every day side by side, with streak boxes under each day.
Home brings your projects out of the tool bag.

Controls
  • WASD walk · Shift run
  • Space/E fly up · Q down
  • Drag to look · scroll to zoom (3rd person / overview)
  • H Home · 123 or V change view · F power up
  • Click the calendar → Year / Overview / Reminders / To-do / Bring to front, − / +, or drag the blue corner handle to resize
  • Click a month's name to bring it to the front · click a day for its summary (Enter saves) · click its dots for reminders · Esc closes
  • Events: drag across days (or Shift+click a second day) to make a multi-day event · click an event bar to edit it
  • Click the tool bag to open or close it · click a project card for details
  • VR: press VR (bottom right)
  • Left stick walk · right stick turn / fly up & down
  • Trigger clicks the calendar, months, days and buttons · hold Grip and drag to move the calendar
  • Trigger-hold the blue corner handle and raise/lower your hand to resize
  • A/B bigger / smaller calendar · X change view · Y power up
